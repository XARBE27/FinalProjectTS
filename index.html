About Chess

Chess is a timeless strategy game that challenges the mind and sharpens decision-making skills. Its origins date back to around the 6th century in India, where an early form of the game called chaturanga was played. From there, it spread to Persia, becoming shatranj, and later reached Europe during the Middle Ages, where the modern rules of chess began to take shape by the 15th century. Played between two opponents, each controlling an army of pieces with unique movements, the goal is simple yet profound: checkmate the opponent’s king. Beneath its straightforward rules lies a world of deep tactics, long-term planning, and creativity. Every move matters, as players must balance offense and defense while predicting their opponent’s intentions. From casual games to intense competitive matches, chess offers endless possibilities and teaches patience, focus, and resilience. Today, it is played worldwide both on physical boards and online, uniting millions of players in a shared passion for strategy and intellect. Whether you’re a beginner or a seasoned player, the board is always ready for your next move.
