Sicilian Defence Basics

The Sicilian Defence begins with:

1. e4 c5

Black immediately fights for the center from the side while creating active counterplay and unbalanced positions.

This opening is known for aggressive play, tactical battles, and dynamic attacking chances for both sides.

Many grandmasters choose the Sicilian Defence because it is ambitious, flexible, and one of the strongest responses to 1. e4.

Main Ideas

Dynamic Counterplay

1. e4 c5
  • Creates active positions
  • Fights for the center
  • Strong attacking chances

Why Play It?

  • Very aggressive defense
  • Popular at top level
  • Creates winning chances
  • Excellent against 1. e4
