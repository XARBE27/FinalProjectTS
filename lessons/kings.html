King's Gambit Basics

The King's Gambit begins with:

1. e4 e5
2. f4

White sacrifices a pawn to quickly gain control of the center and launch aggressive attacks against Black's king.

This opening is known for sharp tactics, fast development, and exciting attacking chances from the very beginning.

Many attacking players choose the King's Gambit because it creates dangerous positions, open lines for the pieces, and thrilling games.

Main Ideas

Aggressive Attack
