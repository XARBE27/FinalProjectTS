Queen's Gambit Basics

The Queen's Gambit begins with:

1. d4 d5
2. c4

White offers a pawn to challenge Black's center and gain strong control of important central squares.

This opening is known for strategic play, solid development, and long-term positional pressure throughout the game.

Many grandmasters choose the Queen's Gambit because it is powerful, reliable, and creates strong opportunities in both the middlegame and endgame.

Main Ideas

Center Control

1. d4 d5
2. c4
  • Challenges Black's center
  • Strong positional pressure
  • Flexible piece development

Why Play It?
